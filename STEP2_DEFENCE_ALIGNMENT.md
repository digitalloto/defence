## STRATEGIC ALIGNMENT: VISION 2047 PRIORITIES → AIMCRS CAPABILITIES

### PRIORITY 1: DATA-CENTRIC WARFARE & DECISION SUPERIORITY

**Vision 2047 states:** *"Transit from information superiority to decision superiority (Net Centric to Data Centric Warfare)."*

**AIMCRS delivers:**
- NETRA processes multi-source intelligence (SIGINT, HUMINT, OSINT, GEOINT) through AI algorithms to produce actionable decision support — not raw data dumps
- Real-time threat assessment enables commanders to act on processed intelligence, not raw information
- Automated threat prioritisation reduces the OODA loop (Observe-Orient-Decide-Act cycle — the speed at which a commander can make decisions) from hours to seconds
- Directly enables the doctrinal shift from Net Centric to Data Centric operations

**Phase alignment:** Phase I (2025–2030) — Immediate deployment readiness
