## D. TIPS FOR USING THESE MESSAGES

1. **Always personalise** — Use their real name, rank, and title. Never send a generic message.
2. **Reference Vision 2047 specifically** — This shows you've done your homework and you're aligned with national priorities.
3. **Keep WhatsApp messages short** — Defence officials are busy. Get to the point in 3-4 paragraphs.
4. **LinkedIn is more formal** — You can be slightly more detailed here.
5. **Always offer a specific ask** — "15-minute demo" is better than "let's connect sometime."
6. **Follow up once after 5-7 days** — Use Message 4 (the short follow-up). Don't follow up more than twice.
7. **Never make claims you can't demonstrate** — If they say yes to a demo, you must be ready to show the system.
8. **Use "Sir/Ma'am"** — Always. This is expected in Indian defence culture.
9. **Don't send these to too many people at the same organisation** — Pick 1-2 key contacts per organisation.
10. **Time your messages** — Send WhatsApp messages between 10 AM and 6 PM IST. Never on Sundays.
